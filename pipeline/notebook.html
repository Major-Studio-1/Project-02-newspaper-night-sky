Notebook

How this project was made, told as it happened: what we wanted to know, what we decided and why, the wrong turns, the difficulties and how we got past them, and the care taken for the people who visit and for the people in the papers.

Each entry is dated, says what it changed, and links to the place on the site where that change lives. The quotes in gold are the maker's own words. Choose a kind to read one thread.
