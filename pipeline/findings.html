Open questions

How much was false news?A correction can be counted; a false report cannot be known as false without a record of the true one. Without that denominator no rate can be given.
Who copied whom?Shared wording shows that papers printed the same text, not where each took it from. The site draws a copying route only where a paper names its source.
Is a share of the papers a share of attention?No. These are shares of what survives and was reprinted; how much of each year survives varies more than tenfold, so finding 1 comes first.
Why do things come back?The data says when: fever in the late summer, the embargo with each crisis. Why is a question for the history behind the papers.
