Story telescope v1 · 7 Nov 2026

Every story printed in at least two newspapers of this archive, 1770–1810, as a star.

How to read it. Left to right is the date a story was first printed. Up and down is the average latitude of the towns whose papers printed it, north at the top (scattered slightly, by up to a tenth of a degree, so stories printed by the same papers don't stack into one line). Bigger, brighter stars were printed in more newspapers. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move through time, and click a star to open it in the pulse view with its printings and passages.

Brightest stories in view

    Matched stories were found automatically by shared wording across newspapers and are not checked against the scans; a match can join neighbouring items, and printings in damaged transcriptions are missed. Reviewed stories were read and checked; a reviewed story usually also appears as a matched star. Positions follow the surviving archive: which papers survive shapes where stars can appear, so an empty region is not proof that nothing was printed.
