Each pulse marks one printed passage on its publication date. Reviewed stories were read and checked against the scans; matched stories were found automatically by shared wording and are not checked. In a single story, colours describe textual roles; in the reviewed overview, colours identify story categories. Glow is an animation effect: papers that printed the same story within 40 days glow again together. Constellation lines appear only when you pick a story; they group its printings by nearness on the map and never show who copied whom. Nodes are placed roughly by town of publication, not to scale. More on what a pulse shows. Each matched printing says whether its passage is in both machine transcriptions of its page; an audit of 200 matched stories found that about 1 passage in 5, up to 1 in 3, has a misreading that changes a fact, so check the scan before quoting.
